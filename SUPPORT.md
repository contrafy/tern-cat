# Getting help

- **How do I ...?** Check [docs/usage.md](docs/usage.md) and
  [docs/configuration.md](docs/configuration.md) first, then open a
  [question issue](https://github.com/contrafy/tern-cat/issues/new?template=question.md).
- **Something is broken.** Open a
  [bug report](https://github.com/contrafy/tern-cat/issues/new?template=bug_report.md) with your
  tern-cat and Tern versions, OS and steps to reproduce. Remove personal paths, hostnames and
  command lines from any logs you paste.
- **Ideas.** Open a
  [feature request](https://github.com/contrafy/tern-cat/issues/new?template=feature_request.md).
- **Sprite and sound packs.** Use the
  [sprite pack](https://github.com/contrafy/tern-cat/issues/new?template=sprite_pack.md)
  template to list a pack or report a pack format problem.
- **Security.** Never in a public issue: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Tern itself** (crashes, sign-in, the plugin API): see the
  [Tern docs](https://docs.stencil.so/tern/); problems with Tern are not tern-cat bugs.
